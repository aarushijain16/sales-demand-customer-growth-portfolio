End-to-End Apparel Business Analytics

Built on synthetic data modelled on a real B2B apparel manufacturer — replicating actual demand patterns, SKU variability, and distributor payment cycles. Modelled a ~$60M potential working capital opportunity via DSO simulation, identified an ~11.7% demand-supply gap, and flagged mid-price segments ($240–$480) as key untapped revenue drivers. Built Holt-Winters demand forecasting framework (MAPE: 3.09%) and four live Power BI dashboards connected to PostgreSQL.

Optimising Inventory and Reducing Stock-Outs Using ABC-XYZ and Demand Planning

Applied ABC-XYZ segmentation to a public retail dataset to classify ~98% of SKUs as slow-moving or dead stock, modelling ~$15M+ in potential stock-out losses and ~$10M+ in excess inventory. Built a demand-driven replenishment framework using safety stock modelling and reorder point calculations to eliminate reactive inventory decisions and surface the working capital case for a full inventory strategy overhaul.

📈 Forecasting and Pricing

Market Demand Forecasting and Pricing Intelligence Using Proxy Data (Airbnb Research)

MSc dissertation selected for academic publication with Edward Elgar Publishing (2025) — one of few analyst portfolios backed by peer-reviewed research. Engineered proxy demand signals (reviews, availability, pricing trends) to forecast market demand and pricing dynamics where direct booking data was unavailable. Applied XGBoost, Random Forest, and Decision Tree models on London Airbnb listing data (2008–2023), with XGBoost delivering the best forecasting performance. Identified ~25% Inner London price premium and listing type as the strongest demand driver.
